# How Money Works — timestamped evidence library

This library supports the reconstruction report. It records the 18-video corpus and the observations used to infer the current production grammar. Timestamps are linked to the original videos. The analysis used locally downloaded 360p review copies, English captions, 10-second contact sheets, 2-second sequence sheets, FFmpeg scene detection, and targeted 0.5-second motion strips.

## Quantitative measurements

- 18 videos; 15,571 seconds total.
- Weighted narration rate: **196.7 words per minute**.
- Median per-video rate: **196.0 WPM**.
- Median reliable sentence length: **21 words**.
- Current five-video sample: **8.9 conservative hard cuts/minute** (FFmpeg scene score > 0.30).
- Full corpus: **7.7 conservative hard cuts/minute**.
- Median interval between detected hard cuts: **3.37 seconds**.
- Manual 2-second opening samples show **10–16 meaningful visual changes/minute** and **4–7 major template changes/minute**; the gap comes from internal reveals that do not replace the template.
- Silence detection at -40 dB with 250 ms minimum found median pauses of **0.34–0.36 seconds**. Long pauses above one second are rare.
- Measured integrated loudness of representative uploads: **-17.6 to -19.4 LUFS**, true peaks **+0.18 to -2.87 dBTP**, loudness range **3.4–6.7 LU**. Public-platform playback normalization may alter perceived loudness.

## Method limitations

- Exact font files, project files, character source footage, music stems, and template source assets are not public.
- The 360p review copies are adequate for composition, timing, palette estimation, and motion analysis, but fine kerning, grain, and sub-pixel measurements are approximate.
- Scene detection under-counts dissolves, graphic changes inside a persistent layout, highlights, chart-line reveals, and pose changes.
- Auto-caption punctuation is unreliable in six videos, so sentence metrics use the twelve reliable caption tracks.
- Observations distinguish measured facts from implementation recommendations; inferred production choices are identified as such in the main report.
