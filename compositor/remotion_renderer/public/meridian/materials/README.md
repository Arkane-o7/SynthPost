# Meridian material library

These are original, channel-specific production surfaces used by the Meridian
Remotion templates. They are intentionally separate from SynthPost and Beyond
branding and render assets.

- `editorial-desk-wide.webp` — top-down research desk with edge props and a
  clear central composition zone.
- `paper-crumpled-ivory.webp` — subtle flattened-paper fibers and creases for
  clipping, document, and card surfaces.
- `cork-natural.webp` — detailed cork backing for evidence maps and causal
  diagrams.

The source images were generated specifically for this project and compressed
to high-quality WebP for predictable local rendering. Motion is applied in
Remotion; no third-party GIF or channel artwork is bundled.
