# Story source and boundaries

This pilot does not make factual claims that need external research. It is a
fictional composite built from a common digital mishap: sending a private message
to the wrong group.

Production boundaries:

- No event is presented as something that happened to the user.
- No real employer, messaging service, manager, or coworker is identified.
- Dialogue is authored for this fictional story, not represented as a transcript.
- The joke stays on the narrator's own overconfidence and panic.
- The interface is a generic chat drawing, not a trademarked product replica.
- Visuals are original procedural animation. No frames or character designs from
  existing storytime-animation creators are used.

## Narrative arc

1. Cold open: the message is already sent and listeners are appearing.
2. Rewind: the narrator records a harmless private complaint about an icebreaker.
3. Mistake: two adjacent chat names and one careless tap.
4. Panic: delete, apology, and eleven listens make everything worse.
5. Aftermath: the team is painfully polite the next morning.
6. Button: the group gets a new warning name, which mostly works.

## Density and visual rhythm

- Cold open: Impact
- Rewind/setup: Standard
- Wrong-chat reveal: Impact
- Damage control: Standard
- Next morning: Standard
- Final callback: Impact
