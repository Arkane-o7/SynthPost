# Visual direction

Reading this as an awkward everyday story for a YouTube storytime audience, Playful / Agency mode - VARIANCE 8 / MOTION 8 / DENSITY 3.

- Theme: warm paper with dark plum ink; Sidequest violet is the sole primary accent, supported by locked orange, coral, mint, and sky coding colors.
- Typography: Anton Local for display; Inter/system sans for bubbles and small labels.
- Motion: bouncy pose entrances, character bob, panel movement, and held punchlines. No flashes, strobes, or light/dark theme changes.
- Rhythm: hook, staging, dialogue, imagination, button; dense three-panel montages are always followed by a simpler character or punchline frame.
- Assets: zero external media. All imagery is original procedural line animation.
