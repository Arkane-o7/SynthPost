# Topic direction

- Channel: Sidequest (`storytime`)
- Working title: I Sent the Voice Note to the Entire Group Chat
- Format: original limited-animation storytime pilot
- Story status: clearly fictional composite created to validate the pipeline
- Audience: general YouTube audience, teens and adults
- Language: English
- Tone: casual, self-aware, lightly awkward; never cruel
- Duration target: 1.5–2.5 minutes
- Canvas: horizontal 16:9, designed at 1920×1080 and final-capable at 4K
- Visual approach: procedural Sidequest cast and locations; no third-party art or stock footage
- Narrator: provisional channel-owned synthetic pilot voice
- Review rule: preview and key frames first; final 4K render only after explicit approval

The story follows a fictional narrator who records a private voice note about
an office team-building day and accidentally sends it to the office group chat.
The comedy comes from the narrator's own assumptions, deletion attempts, and
the next morning's social aftermath.
