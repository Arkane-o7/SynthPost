# Meridian prototype: The bank loan that left the bank

- Channel: Meridian
- Audience: financially curious general viewers
- Format: short editorial explainer
- Tone: precise, skeptical, lightly dry
- Target runtime: 80–95 seconds
- Editorial question: Why did private credit grow so quickly, and did the risk really leave the banking system?
- Visual test: structured headline clipping, pinned evidence, presenter-safe annotation network, social clipping, official chart/document, and two-source footage montage
